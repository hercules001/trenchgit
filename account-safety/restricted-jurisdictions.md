# Restricted Jurisdictions

* **United States:** Prohibited for residents, citizens, and entities across all US states and territories.
* **Ontario, Canada:** Prohibited for residents, citizens, and entities located in Ontario. Other Canadian jurisdictions are not specifically named as restricted.
* **Sanctioned & export-controlled jurisdictions:** Access is restricted for jurisdictions subject to applicable economic sanctions or export-control laws. This may include **North Korea, Iran, Cuba, Syria, Russia**, and certain Russian-occupied Ukrainian regions, including **Crimea, Donetsk, and Luhansk**.

> **Important:** Restrictions may also apply based on citizenship, residency, location, incorporation, or applicable laws and regulations. Users are responsible for determining whether they are eligible to access Trench.
