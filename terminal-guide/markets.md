# Markets

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-08-21 at 08.26.07@2x.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Explore global markets, track what's moving, and quickly find the assets you want to trade.** The Markets page brings together live prices, market performance, volume, open interest, funding, and personalized watchlists in one place.

| Section               | Description                                                                            |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Watchlist**         | Keep your favorite markets in one place for quick access.                              |
| **Market Overview**   | View the selected market's price, 24h change, volume, open interest, and funding rate. |
| **Top Gainers**       | See the markets with the strongest positive price movement.                            |
| **Top Losers**        | Find markets experiencing the largest negative price movements.                        |
| **New**               | Discover newly available markets on Trench.                                            |
| **Market Categories** | Filter markets by **Crypto, Equities, Indices, Commodities,** or **Forex**.            |
| **Market Table**      | Compare markets using price, 24h change, funding, volume, and open interest.           |
| **Search**            | Quickly find a specific market by name or ticker.                                      |

### Actions

#### Add a Market to Your Watchlist

Click the **⭐ icon** next to any market to add it to your Watchlist. Click it again to remove the market.

You can also click the star next to the selected asset at the top of the page.

#### View a Market

Click on a market from the table or Watchlist to open its market overview and view its live price, chart, funding, volume, and open interest.

#### Start Trading

Click **Trade** next to any market to open that asset directly in the Futures trading interface.

#### Filter Markets

Use the category tabs to quickly narrow the list to **Crypto, Equities, Indices, Commodities,** or **Forex**.

#### Find a Market

Use the **Search markets** field to find an asset by its name or ticker.

#### Track What's Moving

Use **Top Gainers** and **Top Losers** to quickly identify markets experiencing the biggest moves, then open the market or trade directly from the list.

#### Understand the Key Metrics

**Tip:** Use the Markets page to scan what's moving first, then open the asset to review its market conditions before entering a trade.
