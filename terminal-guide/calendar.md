# Calendar

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-08-21 at 08.24.10@2x.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Know what's coming before it moves the market.** Track upcoming earnings and key economic events to anticipate potential volatility, understand what's driving price movements, and plan your trades around important market dates.

<table><thead><tr><th width="290.359375">Calendar</th><th>Description</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td><strong>Earnings Calendar</strong></td><td>Track upcoming earnings releases from major companies and identify dates that could bring increased volatility to their stocks.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Economic Calendar</strong></td><td>Follow key economic events and data releases such as inflation, interest rates, employment, and GDP that can influence markets globally.</td></tr></tbody></table>

**Why it matters:** Knowing _when_ important events are happening helps you prepare for volatility and understand _why_ a market may be moving.
