# Common Errors

<table><thead><tr><th width="188.27734375">Error / Warning</th><th width="286.85546875">What it means</th><th>What to do</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td><strong>Insufficient Margin</strong></td><td>You don't have enough available collateral to open or increase a position.</td><td>Reduce your position size or add more USDC collateral.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Insufficient Available Balance</strong></td><td>Your available balance is too low after accounting for existing positions, orders, or fees.</td><td>Reduce the order size or close/cancel other orders.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Order Rejected</strong></td><td>The order could not be accepted by the trading engine or market.</td><td>Review your order size, price, leverage, and available margin before trying again.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Invalid Price</strong></td><td>The order price is outside the allowed range or does not meet the market's requirements.</td><td>Adjust your limit or trigger price.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Invalid Order Size</strong></td><td>The requested size does not meet the market's minimum or size increment.</td><td>Adjust the order to the supported size.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Leverage Too High</strong></td><td>Your selected leverage exceeds the maximum available for that market.</td><td>Lower your leverage and try again.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Reduce-Only Order Rejected</strong></td><td>A reduce-only order would exceed your existing position or increase your exposure.</td><td>Reduce the order size or check your current position.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Open Interest Cap Reached</strong></td><td>The market has reached its maximum allowed open interest, restricting additional exposure.</td><td>Reduce the order size, wait for OI to decrease, or try again later.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Position Size Limit</strong></td><td>Your order would exceed the maximum position size allowed for the market.</td><td>Reduce your position size or close part of your existing position.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Order Would Increase Position</strong></td><td>A reduce-only order would increase or reverse your current position instead of reducing it.</td><td>Adjust the order so it only closes your existing position.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Order Not Filled</strong></td><td>A limit, stop-limit, TWAP, or scale order has not found a matching execution yet.</td><td>Wait for the market to reach your specified conditions or cancel and adjust the order.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Transaction Delayed</strong></td><td>Network congestion or system delays may delay order processing or account updates.</td><td>Wait for the order status to update before submitting another order.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Price Difference</strong></td><td>The displayed price may differ from the final execution price due to market movement, latency, or slippage.</td><td>Use a limit order when you need more control over your execution price.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Funding Rate Changed</strong></td><td>Funding rates can change as market conditions and positioning change.</td><td>Check the current funding rate before opening or holding a position.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Market Temporarily Restricted</strong></td><td>A market may temporarily restrict new positions due to liquidity, open interest, or other market conditions.</td><td>Check the market status and try again later.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Liquidation</strong></td><td>Your position's margin is no longer sufficient to support the position and the position may be automatically closed.</td><td>Monitor your liquidation price and consider reducing leverage, reducing the position, or adding margin.</td></tr></tbody></table>

**Tip:** If an order appears delayed or stuck, check **Open Orders** and **Positions** first before submitting another order. Network delays, market conditions, and differences between displayed and executed prices can occur when interacting with Hyperliquid.
