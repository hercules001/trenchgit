# Fees

### Trading Fees

Trench charges a **0.03% (3 bps)** platform fee on top of Hyperliquid's trading fees.

<table><thead><tr><th width="210.1875">Fee</th><th width="215.6640625">Rate</th><th>Description</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td><strong>Trench</strong></td><td><strong>0.03% (3 bps)</strong></td><td>Trench's platform fee on each trade.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Hyperliquid Taker</strong></td><td><strong>0.045% (4.5 bps)</strong></td><td>Applies when taking liquidity, including supported HL DEX orders.</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Hyperliquid Maker</strong></td><td><strong>0.015% (1.5 bps)</strong></td><td>Applies when providing liquidity with eligible maker orders.</td></tr></tbody></table>

#### Example

For a **$10,000 taker trade**:

* Trench: **$3**
* Hyperliquid: **$4.50**
* **Total: $7.50 (0.075%)**

Maker fees can be lower depending on the applicable Hyperliquid maker rate.
